How to play

  1. Study the object. Tap it to inspect both the obverse and reverse.
  2. Place one pin at the documented mint or printing location, then estimate its date.
  3. Lock both guesses. The map contributes 4,000 points and the date contributes 1,000.

Every object is an approved American Numismatic Society record with a documented date, linked production place, matched front and back images, and public-domain image rights. Incomplete and inauthentic records are excluded.
