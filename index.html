How to play

  1. Study the currency image. Labels, portraits, scripts, and symbols are all clues.
  2. Place one pin where you think it was issued. You can move it before locking.
  3. Score up to 5,000 points based on distance outside the documented accepted area.

The photo comes from Wikimedia Commons, but its file location is never used as the answer. Each target is reviewed as a mint, printing facility, issuing authority city, or issuing region. The method and tolerance are shown after each guess.
